# DataScience_Analytics_Internship
Portfolio of Data Science &amp; Analytics Internship tasks 1-5.

---

## Task Summary

### **Task 1: Iris Dataset Classification**
- **Objective:** Classify iris species based on sepal and petal measurements.
- **Dataset:** [Iris Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uciml/iris)

### **Task 2: Credit Risk Prediction**
- **Objective:** Predict whether a loan applicant is likely to default on a loan.
- **Dataset:** [Loan Prediction Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/altruistdelhite04/loan-prediction-problem-dataset)

### **Task 3: Customer Churn Analysis**
- **Objective:** Predict which customers are likely to churn.
- **Dataset:** [Churn Modelling Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shrutimechlearn/churn-modelling)

### **Task 4: Predicting Insurance Claim Amounts**
- **Objective:** Estimate medical insurance charges based on personal data.
- **Dataset:** [Medical Cost Personal Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mirichoi0218/insurance)

### **Task 5: Personal Loan Acceptance Prediction**
- **Objective:** Predict which customers will accept a personal loan offer.
- **Dataset:** [Bank Marketing Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/janiobachmann/bank-marketing-dataset)
